RetroPal

All-in-one retro emulator for Android & Android TV

One app for your whole classic game library, from 8-bit handhelds to Nintendo 64, PlayStation, Dreamcast, and PSP. Play the games you legally own with save states, rewind, cloud saves, achievements, automatic artwork, multiplayer, and full controller support. Pick a pixel-perfect or modern look, with LCD, composite, and CRT filters — on your phone or from the couch.

Features

Modern conveniences wrapped around faithful emulation.

🏆

RetroAchievements

Unlock and track achievements as you play your favorites, with softcore and hardcore modes and an in-app achievements viewer.

☁️

Cloud Save Backup

Back up your progress to Google Drive or local storage and keep your saves safe across reinstalls and devices.

💾

Import Your Saves

Bring existing save files with you so you never have to start a game over.

⏪

Save States, Rewind & Cheats

Save anywhere with multiple slots and thumbnails, rewind your last few moments, fast-forward through slow parts, and add cheat codes per game.

🖼️

Automatic Artwork

Box art and metadata are fetched automatically to build a beautiful, browsable library.

🎮

Controller-First Controls

Navigate everything with a gamepad and remap buttons and analog sticks separately for every console — or use on-screen touch controls with selectable skins and a layout editor.

👥

Multiplayer

Play with up to 4 controllers on one device, or join friends over the same Wi-Fi network on supported systems.

📺

Android TV Ready

Lean back with D-pad navigation and a layout built for the living room, and play your cartridge-era and arcade collection on the big screen.

🌐

Wireless ROM Transfer

A built-in HTTP server makes moving your game files onto your TV effortless — no cables needed.

✨

Enhanced Graphics

Auto mode sharpens and upscales visuals for a crisp, modern look on high-resolution screens.

🔲

Pixel Perfect Mode

Prefer authenticity? Keep every pixel exactly as it was on the original hardware.

📼

Retro Display Filters

Add a handheld LCD, composite, or CRT look, take full control of aspect ratio, screen size, and position, and pick a theme that suits you.

⚙️

Per-Console Settings

Settings are organized console by console, with BIOS files, video, audio, and performance options for each system, tuned automatically for your device.

💿

Disc & Archive Support

Load CHD, CUE/BIN, ISO, and GDI disc images, multi-disc M3U playlists, and ZIP or 7z archives.

🚀

Improved NDS Core

Custom Nintendo DS enhancements built on a modified melonDS core for smoother play.
